Choose a setup, then start your race.

Mobile: tap Racer setup to customize. Hold GAS to drive forward around the oval; hold REVERSE to back up (at a slower speed); LEFT / RIGHT change your racing lane. Keyboard: ↑ / W to accelerate, ← / → to steer, ↓ / S to brake, R to reverse. BRAKE overrides GAS and slows you to a stop. Hay bales and the CPU car block your path: change lanes to pass. Each full circuit counts as one lap. Race progress is local to this device.

Maker Quest Power Racers is an original, unofficial arcade demonstration. The fictional course, vehicle options, and lap times do not represent a confirmed festival event.
